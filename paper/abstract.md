# Abstract (draft)

We measured the temperature of a mug of freshly brewed coffee every three minutes for one hour at a room
temperature of 21.5 °C. A least-squares fit of Newton's law of cooling to the logarithm of the excess
temperature describes the data well and gives a cooling constant of 0.046 per minute, so the excess over room
temperature halves about every 15 minutes. The coffee reaches 60 °C after roughly 11.5 minutes. A single run
cannot separate the faster cooling by evaporation in the first minutes from the exponential law; repeated
runs, with and without a lid, would.
